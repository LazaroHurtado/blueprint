# Community code of conduct

Blueprint welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels. In issues,
pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces:

- Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Make room for different perspectives and accessibility needs.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate, publish private information, or direct
  sexualized or abusive material at others.
- Respect consent, intellectual property, and the privacy of contributors.
- Accept moderation and technical decisions without personal attacks.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, warn participants, restrict
participation, or remove access when needed. Decisions should be proportionate
and consider the safety of affected people. Maintainers are subject to these
standards too.

Use GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse** controls for private reports;
see [GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not post private incident details in an issue. If the report concerns a
maintainer, use GitHub's reporting/support channels rather than asking that
maintainer to investigate themselves. No project-specific private email address
has been published.

Security vulnerabilities use the separate [security reporting process](SECURITY.md).
